This chapter presents a critical analysis of the experimental results and
positions the proposed framework within the context of certified imitation control approaches.
The discussion is divided into three main aspects, beginning with a qualitative analysis of the interaction 
between imitation learning, neural Lyapunov learning, and the certification algorithm.
This is followed by an evaluation of the theoretical and numerical challenges associated with nonlinear constrained dynamics. 
Subsequently, fundamental limitations regarding dimensionality, expert dataset generation, and model accuracy are analyzed.
