\section{Qualitative Discussion of Results}
\label{sec:discussion-results}

The linear experimental result demonstrates that combining imitation learning with formal neural Lyapunov synthesis 
resolves the safety limitations of uncertified neural policies while retaining near-optimal control performance. 
In contrast to the paper of Yang et al. \cite{yangLyapunovstableNeuralControl2024}, this work shows that it is possible 
to learn a Lyapunov candidate that can be formally verified, while also maintaining near optimum behavior. 
Supervised imitation learning on nominal MPC trajectories provides a strong inductive a priori information,
enabling the policy network to accurately reproduce the expert's behavior, which is reflected in the low median and
mean values of the tracking errors in both benchmarks. 
However, since pure supervised learning lacks guarantees of stability in a closed-loop system,
the subsequent co-training phase specifically refines the policy parameters to form a vector field that decreases 
toward equilibrium without compromising the underlying MPC properties.
