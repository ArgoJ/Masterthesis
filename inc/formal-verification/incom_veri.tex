\section{Incomplete Verification}
\label{sec:incom-verification}
The aim of general formal neural network verification is to prove that a desired specification holds for all inputs within 
a predefined domain $\mathcal{F} \subset \mathbb{R}^{n_0}$. 
Consider a feed-forward neural network as defined in \labelcref{eq:nn-forward} and a scalar specification function 
$\psi(x) \in \mathbb{R}$, where $\psi(x) > 0$ denotes satisfaction of the safety or stability condition. 
From this, the verification problem can be formulated as a global minimization problem:
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:verification-problem}
    f^\star = \min_{x \in \mathcal{F}} \psi(x) 
    \quad \text{s.t.} 
    \quad z^{(l)} = W^{(l)} a^{(l-1)} + b^{(l)}, \quad a^{(l)} = \sigma^{(l)}\!\left(z^{(l)}\right), \quad a^{(0)} = x,  
\end{equation}
for all layers $l \in \{1, \dots, L\}$.
