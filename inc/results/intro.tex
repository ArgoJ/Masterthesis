This chapter presents the experimental evaluation of the proposed learning and formal verification framework. 
To this end, two benchmark systems are selected: the double integrator and the cartpole. 
While the double integrator serves as a low-dimensional linear reference baseline, 
the cartpole represents a nonlinear benchmark to stress-test the framework.
The remainder of this chapter starts with the general setup in \cref{sec:system-modeling}, 
where the systems and the hyperparameters are shown. This is followed by the results on 
the linear system in \cref{sec:results-linear}, with a closer look at the performance of the 
imitation policy as well as the performance on the subsequent co-trained policy and its 
verification capabilities.
Finally, \cref{sec:results-nonlinear} discusses the nonlinear cartpole system, analyzing the 
progress of the co-training, the counterexamples, and the results of the formal verification attempts.
